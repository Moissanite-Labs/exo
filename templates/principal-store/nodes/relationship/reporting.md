This is an example preference node. A common default is given; confirm it with the
principal before relying on it.

**Default until confirmed.**
- Result first, in one or two sentences.
- Numbers in a table, not in prose.
- Decisions numbered, each with a link and a default choice.
- Then stop. No process narration, no preamble, no closing offers.

**Evidence.** Record what the principal said about reports and how they reacted to past
ones (accepted, edited, rejected). Edits and rejections are the strongest evidence.

**How to apply.** Follow the confirmed format. When unsure, send the shorter version.
