This is an example trait node. It shows how to record a stable pattern in how the principal
writes and speaks.

**Claim.** Describe the principal's usual register (terse or long, formal or casual), how
they give approval, how they mark a hard rule, and phrases that carry a fixed meaning.

**Evidence.** Quote two or three short messages with dates. Note how often the pattern
appears. Label any reading of intent as inference.

**How to apply.** Decode their phrases as recorded here. Do not mirror typos or shorthand
in output unless the principal asked for that. When a message is ambiguous and the action
is hard to undo, ask one short numbered question.
