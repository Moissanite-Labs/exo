# Exo brand

Status: accepted (D-049, 2026-10-13); revised the same day: the name is "3X0" with the mark as its X, and the mark is
the origin figure's lines without dots (see the D-049 revision notes). The brand page is `site/brand/index.html`; every asset there, the nav
brand on every page, the favicons, the app icons, the og image and the terminal marks are generated by
`scripts/brand_assets.py` (`--png` also renders the PNG icons and `site/og.png` with headless Chrome).

**Naming (principal's ruling, D-063).** The **exosphere** is Exo's evolving 3D graphic. Its forms are **x0** (the egg),
then **x**, then **3xo**. Later forms are still being named; this list stays open. The community exosphere on the
website is x0. The engine file and its identifiers keep the old name (`site/brand/egg.js`, `ExoEgg`, `data-exo-egg`)
so nothing that embeds them breaks; "egg" now means only the x0 form.

## The construction

Everything comes from one drawing, figure 1 of the Incentre lattice origin page
(https://aska-digital.github.io/incentre-lattice/origin.html). Units: the square has side 2, y points up.

1. A square with corners (0, 0) and (2, 2).
2. Both diagonals. They cross at the centre (1, 1) and cut the square into four right isosceles triangles.
3. In each triangle, the three angle bisectors. Every point on a bisector is equally far from the two sides of its
   corner. A base corner is 45°, so its bisector rises at 22.5°.
4. The bisectors meet at the incentre, equally far from all three sides. For the bottom triangle A (0, 0), B (2, 0),
   C (1, 1) it is (1, R) with R = tan 22.5° = √2 − 1 ≈ 0.4142. The incircle has radius R.
5. Each triangle's bisectors run from its two square corners (red on the origin page) and from the centre (amber) to
   its incentre: twelve lines. No dot is drawn; the incentre is where its three lines meet.

| Triangle | Incentre (where the lines meet) | ≈ | Lines from the corners | Line from the centre |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bottom | (1, √2 − 1) | (1, 0.4142) | (0, 0), (2, 0) | (1, 1) |
| Right | (3 − √2, 1) | (1.5858, 1) | (2, 0), (2, 2) | (1, 1) |
| Top, inside the V | (1, 3 − √2) | (1, 1.5858) | (2, 2), (0, 2) | (1, 1) |
| Left | (√2 − 1, 1) | (0.4142, 1) | (0, 2), (0, 0) | (1, 1) |

The generator computes each incentre with the incentre formula, (|BC|·A + |CA|·B + |AB|·C) / perimeter, and asserts it
equals the closed form above and that the base bisector rises at 22.5°. In an icon of size N with margin m, a point
(u, v) is drawn at (m + u·s, N − m − v·s) with s = (N − 2m)/2.

- Tiled, the incentres of four neighbouring squares form a regular octagon: the 4.8.8 tiling.
- Lifted to a cube of side 2, the six incentres sit on the axes at K = 2 − √2 from the centre. Stacked cubes give the
  truncated cubic honeycomb; every edge has length 2R.
- In n dimensions the numbers do not change: 2n dots per cell, each touching 2n − 1 others.

Credit: the figure, and every number above, follows the open research project Incentre lattice
(aska-digital/incentre-lattice, https://aska-digital.github.io/incentre-lattice/). The classical results it rests on
(the incentre, the medial axis, the Wythoff construction as developed by Coxeter, the uniform tilings and honeycombs)
belong to everyone. Exo takes the geometry and the red/amber split of the lines (mapped to its own orange and a soft
amber); that project's colours and type are its own and are not used here.

## The mark

No dots. The twelve bisectors are the focus; the square and the X are the frame, and the X's top half is the orange V.

| Form | Size | What it draws | Files |
|---|---|---|---|
| Large | 96 px and up | Square, X with its V, twelve bisectors, four incircles (centre the incentre, radius √2 − 1, tangent to all three sides) | `mark.svg`, `mark-light.svg`, `app-icon*.svg`, icons 180 to 1024, the og image |
| Small | under 96 px | Square, X with its V, twelve bisectors | `mark-small.svg`, `mark-small-light.svg`, `favicon.svg`, `favicon-32.png` |
| 16 px | 16 px | As small, with the square on the tile's outer pixel ring and 1 px lines | `favicon-16.svg`, `favicon-16.png` |

The circle threshold, 96 px, comes from actual-size renders at 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96 and 128 px: at 48 px the circles
are a smudge, at 64 px they are visible but crowd the lines, at 96 px they sit clear (`CIRCLES_MIN` in the
generator). At 16 px the twelve lines and the X merge into an orange star in a square; the V cannot be picked out.
The favicon keeps the lines as asked; the plain X with its V is the fallback if that is not wanted.

Colours, on dark: square `#8b8b86`; the X's lower legs `#b4b3ad`; the V `#ff5b14`, a little heavier than the lines.
Large: lines from the corners `#ff5b14`, lines from the centre and the incircles amber `#f2b25c` (circles at 60%).
Small: all twelve lines in one tint, accent-2 `#ff8a4c`, which reads cleaner than two colours at small sizes and leaves
the V as the strongest orange. On light: square `#8b8b86`, legs `#5a5a56`, amber `#d98a2b`.

## The 3X0 rule

The exosphere's forms are named x0 (the egg), then x, then 3xo. The wordmark spells the last: "3X0". Its X is the mark.

1. The X is drawn as the mark without its square (the "open" form): the X with its orange V, the twelve bisectors and,
   when large, the incircles. The figure's square is the X's centre-line box, so the bisectors start exactly at the
   centres of the X's flat ends. The X stroke is 0.6 of the letter stroke and the lines 0.24 (0.17 when large), so the
   line work carries the glyph's weight. Size rules: incircles from 96 px tall, the X and its twelve lines from 24 px,
   and under 24 px, where the lines blur, the plain X with its V (`wordmark-facet-*.svg`).
2. The 3 is the E drawn as a mirrored E: three arms off a spine on the right, flat ends on the left, the middle arm
   shorter (it stops at 0.3 of the width). Its right corners are cut at 45°, inside and out, by the same cut as the
   zero, so its right side reads as the round of a 3.
3. The 0 is a narrow octagon, 0.7 of its height wide. Its corner cut is w(1 − R)/2, so its top is w·R wide: the
   incentre number. The inner cut lies one stroke width inside the outer one.
4. The plain X: two strokes at exactly 45° with flat horizontal terminals; its top half owns the crossing and is
   `#ff5b14`, the V, so the name also says "evo". On light, the 3, the 0 and the X's lower legs are `#08090a`.
5. Never outline, stretch, rotate or recolour the V.

| Wordmark | Files | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Open: 3, the mark without its square, 0 | `wordmark-open-{lg,sm}-{dark,light}.svg` | **The pick.** The middle glyph keeps the X's silhouette, so it reads 3X0 at the nav's 24 px and as the figure at display size. The nav brand on every page and the og sign-off |
| Framed: 3, the whole mark, 0 | `wordmark-framed-{lg,sm}-*.svg` | Faithful, but the frame makes the middle letter a box; at 24 px and below it reads as 3, an orange tile, 0 |

Geometry (units): cap height 28, stroke 5, letter gap 4; the 3 and the 0 are 19.6 wide, the X is 28 + 5√2 wide. At
16 px tall the stroke is 2.9 px.

Label form, "3 X 0": the facet glyphs with the plain X, a stroke of 0.18 of the height and a gap of half the height,
for labels and tight UI. In the terminal it is the text `3 X 0` with the
X in orange (a cell cannot be half orange). Plain text in prose stays "Exo".

## The exosphere

`site/brand/egg.js` draws the lattice as the exosphere, a living orb. No dependencies, Canvas 2D, one file. Under
`prefers-reduced-motion` it draws one still frame (a lit path through a few cells) and never animates. It pauses when
off screen or in a background tab. Measured cost: under 0.5 ms of script per frame at stage 4 (510 dots, 2,130 edges).

What moves: packets of information travel along the edges, one hop at a time; a dot they reach lights orange and its
cell glows, with its lines to the cell centre (the drawing's line to the centre). A heartbeat in the core cell sends
packets out. Activity sets the beat rate and the background traffic.

### Stages

| Stage | Name | Dimension | Cells | Dots | Shell | Brightness |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | egg | 3 | 1 (the yolk: an octahedron) | 6 | closed | 0.62 |
| 1 | hatchling | 3 | 7 (plus the face cells) | 42 | cracking, fading | 0.74 |
| 2 | lattice | 3 | 19 (plus the edge cells); the first cube drawn | 114 | gone | 0.86 |
| 3 | hyper | 4 | 33; the fourth axis unfolds out of the third | 264 | gone | 1 |
| 4 | spare | 5 | 51 | 510 | gone | 1 |

Stages are a float from 0 to 4. Each element appears over one unit of stage, so 1.5 is halfway between hatchling and
lattice. Dimension is continuous too: from stage 2 to 3 the fourth coordinate is scaled from 0 to 1, so the 4D cells
grow out of the 3D ones. The shell is the same honeycomb, one ring further out, pushed onto an egg shape; its edges drop
out as it hatches. Cells are the lattice cells with |c|² ≤ 2 in integer cell coordinates.

### API

```js
// <script src="/brand/egg.js"></script>  defines window.ExoEgg
var egg = ExoEgg.mount(canvas, {
  stage: 0,            // 0..4, float
  activity: 0.5,       // 0..1
  seed: 7,             // random seed, for repeatable traffic
  shell: true,         // false hides the egg shell
  spin: 1,             // rotation speed multiplier
  reducedMotion: undefined,  // true/false overrides the media query
  layout: function (W, H) { return { cx: W / 2, cy: H / 2, r: Math.min(W, H) * 0.4 }; }
});
egg.setStage(2);                    // eases there at 0.7 stages per second
egg.setStage(2, { immediate: true });
egg.getStage();                     // the current, possibly in-between, stage
egg.setActivity(0.8);
egg.pulse(6);                       // send 6 packets out of the core (a turn starts, a tool runs)
egg.pause(); egg.resume(); egg.destroy();
ExoEgg.STAGES;                      // the table above, as data
ExoEgg.MAX_STAGE;                   // 4
```

Repository events (proposed, D-059): `egg.event(type, {replay})` with `issue`, `pr`, `merge` or `release`, and
`egg.signal(level)`; the contract is `docs/LIVE-EGG.md` section 1. `pulse(n)` keeps its meaning.

Without script: `<canvas data-exo-egg data-stage="2" data-activity="0.6" data-seed="3" data-shell="off">` is mounted
on load (`ExoEgg.autoMount(scope)` mounts canvases added later). The canvas fills its CSS box.

For exopet: map the evolution line onto stages 0 to 3 (4 is spare) and drive `activity` and `pulse` from real events.
Keep the stage meaning (more cells, more structure, a dimension, more light) so the pet and the brand stay one figure.

## Terminal

`tui-mark.txt` (12 × 6 braille cells) is the small mark in braille: the square, the X and its twelve bisectors. `tui-wordmark.txt` is the label form as text, `3 X 0`. `tui-egg.txt` (14 × 7) is the stage-0 egg. Each
has an `.html` twin with the orange cells (the V; the X) in `<b>`.

The terminal client reads `tui-mark.html` and `tui-wordmark.html` at run time (`clients/tui/exo_tui/brand.py`; the
`.txt` files are the fallback; an installed client uses copies packaged in `exo_tui/brand/`, kept identical by a test).
The boot screen shows the mark and the wordmark; the brand bar and the empty-start card show the wordmark. The
exosphere (x0, the egg at stage 0) is drawn by `exo_tui/exosphere.py` from the same geometry as `egg.js`: large on the
start screen and small in the bottom-right corner for the whole session (D-062, `docs/UI.md`). `tui-egg.txt` stays as
the original still proposal.
